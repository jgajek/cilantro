# Historical tool comparisons

Earlier comparisons with other Reactor tools are preserved in [the benchmark notes](history/pre-0.10/parity.md). They describe particular samples, tool revisions, configurations, and operating systems. They have not been rerun for the 0.10.0 release and should not be read as current product rankings.

For adoption decisions, use CILantro's [current support statement](compatibility.md) and evaluate the relevant samples in your own workflow. Recovery, output readability, VM reconstruction, and behavioral validation are separate comparison dimensions.
